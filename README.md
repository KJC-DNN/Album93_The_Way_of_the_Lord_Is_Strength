# Album 93 — The Way of the Lord Is Strength

Seven bilingual songs. The way of the Lord is strength to the upright and ruin to the wicked. A false balance is an abomination. A gracious woman retains honour. Instruction is loved by the wise. The foolish woman is a snare. A wise son hears his father. Whoever despises the Word shall be destroyed; whoever fears the commandment shall be rewarded.
